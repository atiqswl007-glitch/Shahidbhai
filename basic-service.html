Basic Service

Keep Your Air Conditioner in Top Shape with Our Basic Cleaning Service

Over time, dust and dirt can build up inside your air conditioner, affecting both its performance and the quality of air in your space. Our Basic Service is expertly designed to tackle this, ensuring your air conditioner stays efficient and effective.

Our skilled technicians thoroughly clean filters, inspect for noise issues, and conduct a health check for both indoor and outdoor units. This regular maintenance keeps your AC running smoothly while delivering a refreshing, comfortable atmosphere.

Items Covered in the Basic Service

  • Filter Cleaning: Ensures optimal airflow and reduces dust.
  • Air Conditioner Cleaning: Light cleaning to remove surface dust.
  • Noise Issue Checking: Inspect unusual sounds.
  • Problem Identification: Detect issues and recommend solutions.
  • Indoor & Outdoor Health Check: Complete system inspection.

Benefits of Basic Service

  • Improved Air Quality
  • Smooth & Quiet Operation
  • Preventive Maintenance
