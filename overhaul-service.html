Overhaul Service

Comprehensive Overhaul Service for Peak AC Performance

Give your air conditioner a fresh start with our Overhaul Service. Over time, dust and dirt build up inside, making it work harder and cool less effectively. Our skilled team will carefully take apart, clean, and inspect every part of your AC, fixing any small issues along the way.

This service is ideal for air conditioners that are used a lot or haven’t been cleaned in a long time. With a deep clean and tune-up, we’ll help your AC run more efficiently, use less energy, and last longer— giving you cool, clean air every day!

Items Covered in Overhaul Cleaning

  • Complete Disassembly: Full access to hard-to-reach areas for deep cleaning.
  • Chemical Cleaning: Removal of stubborn grime, mould, and bacteria.
  • Evaporator & Condenser Coils Cleaning: Ensures efficient heat exchange.
  • Blower & Fan Cleaning: Improves air circulation and cooling performance.
  • Drain Pan & Drainage System Clearing: Prevents leakage issues.
  • Thermostat Calibration: Accurate temperature control.
  • Comprehensive Inspection: Identifying and fixing underlying issues.

Benefits of Overhaul Cleaning

  • Maximum Cooling Efficiency: Strong and consistent cooling performance.
  • Prolonged Lifespan: Helps avoid premature replacement costs.
  • Enhanced Air Quality: Cleaner and healthier indoor air.
  • Optimal Energy Savings: Lower electricity consumption and bills.
