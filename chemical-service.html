Deep Clean Your Air Conditioner with Our Chemical Cleaning Service

Experience the ultimate rejuvenation for your air conditioner with our Chemical Cleaning service. Over time, your cooling system accumulates stubborn dirt, mould, and grime, hindering its performance and compromising air quality.

Our experts employ advanced chemical cleaning techniques to provide a deep and thorough cleanse. This helps restore your AC’s efficiency and ensures a refreshing breeze.

Items Covered in Chemical Service

  • Condenser Coils Cleaning: Enhances cooling efficiency and saves energy.
  • Evaporator Coils Cleaning: Removes dirt for optimal airflow and cooling.
  • Chemical Solution Application: Dissolves stubborn grime, mould, and bacteria.
  • Drain Pan & Drainage Clearing: Prevents water leakage issues.

  • Inspection & Troubleshooting: Identifies potential issues and provides recommendations.
